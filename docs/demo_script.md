# Potion Pantry: 6-Step Live Demo Script

**Speaker**: Person C (The Face)  
**Target Duration**: 75 seconds during Slide 4 of the pitch  
**Screen**: Browser showing `http://127.0.0.1:8000` (or local `frontend/index.html`)

---

### Step 1: Establish the Calm Baseline (10s)
> *"Judges, take a look at our Apothecary dashboard right now. It is a calm Tuesday morning. For our primary ingredient, Moonflower, we have 400 units in stock. Our baseline sales run at 100 units per day, giving us 4.0 days of cover. Because our supplier takes 2 days to deliver, our status is solid green — we are completely safe."*

---

### Step 2: The Catalyst — The Festival Strikes (10s)
> *(Action: Click the big golden button **"🌕 Trigger Full Moon Festival"**)*  
> *"Now, the kingdom announces the Full Moon Festival. Demand surges overnight."*

---

### Step 3: The Immediate Forecast Jump (15s)
> *(Action: Point to the big Chart.js visual on the left)*  
> *"Instantly, our forecasting engine detects the spike. You can see our 30-day steady history in purple, and looking forward, the projected demand line jumps from 100 all the way to 300 units per day."*

---

### Step 4: The Critical Alarm (15s)
> *(Action: Point to the pulsing red Moonflower card and top red banner)*  
> *"Look at what happens to our inventory health. At 300 units a day, our 400 units will run dry in exactly 1.3 days. But our supplier takes 2.0 days to deliver! That means without intervention, the shop is guaranteed to be completely out of stock for nearly a full day. The dashboard turns bright red with a critical alert."*

---

### Step 5: The Mathematically Defensible Solution (15s)
> *(Action: Point to the Reorder Table)*  
> *"Instead of guessing or blindly tripling our order, our reorder engine calculates the exact inventory required. It factors in lead time, 3 days of cover buffer, plus 95% confidence safety stock to protect against variance. The system automatically prescribes ordering **1,170 units of Moonflower**."*

---

### Step 6: The Punchline — Advance Announcement Power (10s)
> *"And because we support advance festival announcements, if we know about the festival one week early, this order is scheduled ahead of time so the potion jars arrive before the crowd gathers. No stockouts, no wasted money, and happy customers."*
